What is this app for?

The XC Train Manager App provides practical tools, templates and calculators to support common day-to-day tasks.

It includes delay reporting, late book-off messages, passenger counts, incident notes, reservations messages, BTP 61016 messages, defect reporting, a notepad, commission / scan calculations, Delay Repay and a TIR Builder.

The forms are designed to help capture key information consistently. They support, but do not replace, company procedures and formal reporting systems.

Saving and clearing your information

Most form pages include a 💾 Save icon in the top-right corner. This saves the information entered on that page within the app so it can be restored when you return.

A 🗑️ Clear icon removes the saved and current information after asking you to confirm that you are sure.

Save and Clear controls are not provided on the Commission / Scan Calculator, Delay Repay or Help / Guide pages.

Using the forms

Complete the relevant fields and leave optional fields blank when they are not applicable.

Use the Main Menu button to return to the Home Page.

Where available, use Copy to place the generated report or message on your clipboard, Email to prepare it in your email app, or Print / Save PDF to create a printable or PDF version.

Always check generated information before sending or submitting it. Make sure names, times, dates, headcodes, locations and other operational details are correct.

🚆 Report Delay to Line Manager

Use this page to prepare a delay report for your line manager. Enter the date, headcode, minutes delayed, location and any relevant supporting information.

The delay indicator uses green for under 10 minutes, yellow for 11–29 minutes and red for 30 minutes or more.

⏰ Book Off Late

Use this page when late running means you require a late book-off. Enter the date, job number, original book-off time, delay, reason and any comments.

The app calculates the revised book-off time and prepares the information for copying, emailing or printing where available.

👥 Passenger Counts

Use this page to record manual passenger counts. Each train entry records the date, headcode, station, First Class count and Standard count.

Use ＋ Add Additional Train when recording more than one train. Passenger count email subjects include the date and all entered headcodes.

📝 Incident Report Notes

Use this page to record the basic facts of an incident that may require a formal operational report later.

It is intended as a quick note and prompt and does not replace the formal incident reporting process.

🚫 Reservations / Full & Standing

Use this page to prepare a message requesting that reservations are closed and/or that a full-and-standing service is shown, depending on the option selected.

The message can include the headcode, date, train origin and destination, original departure time and any relevant details entered.

🚔 BTP 61016 Message Builder

Use this page to prepare a text message requesting police assistance.

I am a member of train crew on HEADCODE, the TRAIN TIME FROM - TO, next stop is NEXT STOP @ HH:MM. Requesting police assist due to INCIDENT DETAILS.

Check all details carefully before sending the message to BTP.

🔧 Report Defects

Use this page to record non-safety defects.

Enter the headcode and shared six-digit Unit number once. The same Unit number is then used for every defect entered. Add each defect with its relevant PVN and defect details.

Safety-critical defects must always be reported using the appropriate operational procedure.

📓 Notepad

The Notepad is for on-the-day notes and messages. Add, edit or delete notes as required.

Notes can be saved locally using the 💾 Save icon and cleared using the 🗑️ Clear icon after confirmation.

🧮 Commission / Scan Calculator

Enter ticket sales in pounds and the number of scans.

  • 6% of ticket sales
  • £0.02 per scan
  • The combined total

The calculation updates as figures are entered.

💷 Delay Repay

Use this page to show customers the Delay Repay QR code and information.

Customers whose CrossCountry train is delayed by 30 minutes or more to the destination shown on their ticket may be entitled to compensation, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

📋 TIR Builder

The TIR Builder helps capture information and prepare a structured report.

Enter the relevant train information, irregularity, passenger details, answers and operational notes. Use the available Copy, Email and Print / Save PDF options to produce the completed report.

Remember

  • Check every entry before copying, emailing, printing or submitting a report.
  • Use the correct company mailbox, reporting system and operational procedure.
  • This app provides templates and prompts; it does not replace company procedures, formal reporting systems or emergency arrangements.
  • For emergencies or safety-critical matters, follow the appropriate operational process immediately.

💾 Save and 🗑️ Delete / Clear buttons

On most pages, the floating buttons at the top of the screen allow you to save or clear the information currently entered on that page.

  • 💾 Save — saves the current page data to the app's memory so it can be restored later.
  • 🗑️ Delete / Clear — clears the information currently entered on the screen. You will be asked to confirm before the data is cleared.
