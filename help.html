What is this app for?

The XC Staff App provides simple templates, calculators and note-taking tools to assist with common day-to-day tasks while carrying out your duties.

The app includes delay and late book-off messages, passenger counts, incident notes, reservations messages, BTP 61016 messages, non-safety defect reports, a notepad, commission / scan calculations, Delay Repay, and a TIR Builder.

Forms are designed to capture the basic information you are likely to need, helping reduce the risk of important information being missed.

Using the forms

Complete the relevant fields on each page. Where a field is optional, leave it blank when it is not applicable.

Use the Main Menu button on each page to return to the Home Page.

Where available, use Copy to place the completed message on your clipboard or Email / Generate Message to open your default email application, normally Outlook.

Copying and emailing

Copy copies the generated text so you can paste it into the required system, message or email.

Email / Generate Message prepares the subject and body for your default email application. You will normally need to enter the appropriate mailbox address yourself, such as your line manager, Control, Customer Support, Defects or Late Book Off mailbox.

Always check the generated message before sending it. Make sure names, times, headcodes, locations and other operational details are correct.

🚆 Report Delay to Line Manager

Use this page to report a delay to your line manager.

Enter the date, headcode, minutes delayed, location and any other required information. The delay indication uses:

  • Under 10 minutes: green
  • 11–29 minutes: yellow
  • 30 minutes or more: red

⏰ Book Off Late

Use this page when late running means you require a late book-off.

Enter the date, job number, original book-off time, delay in minutes, reason and comments. The app calculates the new book-off time from the original book-off time and the delay.

The generated email includes the headcode, delay, reason, job number, new book-off time and any additional information entered.

👥 Passenger Counts

Use this page to record manual passenger counts.

Each train records the date, headcode, station, First Class count and Standard count. Use ＋ Add Additional Train at the bottom of the page when recording multiple journeys or trains.

Save Locally stores the count information on the device. Clear All asks for confirmation before clearing the page.

📝 Incident Report Notes

This page is a quick way of recording the basic facts at the time of an incident that may require a formal operational incident report later.

It is intended as a quick note / prompt and does not replace the formal operational incident form.

If the incident is potentially linked to an event or fixture, additional details can be recorded, such as football, racing event, festival, number involved, teams involved and impact factors such as alcohol.

🚫 Reservations / Full & Standing

Use this page to prepare a message requesting that reservations are closed and/or that a full and standing service is shown, depending on the option selected.

The generated message includes the headcode, date, train from/to, original departure time and the details entered on the form.

🚔 BTP 61016 Message Builder

Use this page to prepare a text message requesting police assistance.

The generated SMS follows the format:

I am a member of train crew on HEADCODE, the TRAIN TIME FROM - TO, next stop is NEXT STOP @ HH:MM. Requesting police assist due to INCIDENT DETAILS.

The information is taken directly from the form. Check the message carefully before sending it to BTP.

🔧 Report Defects

This page is for non-safety defects that are not listed elsewhere in the app, or when the usual app/process is unavailable.

Enter the headcode, PVN, unit and defect. Press ＋ to add additional defects; each new defect entry asks for its own PVN.

Use the Copy or Email option to send the completed defect report through the appropriate channel.

Safety-critical defects must continue to be reported using the appropriate operational procedure.

📓 Notepad

The Notepad is for on the day notes / messages.

Press ＋ Add Note to create a note. Each note is automatically timestamped and has its own title and free-text area.

Individual notes can be expanded or collapsed using − / + and deleted individually. Save Locally stores the notes on the device and Clear All removes all notes after confirmation.

🧮 Commission / Scan Calculator

Enter ticket sales in £ and the number of scans.

  • 6% of ticket sales
  • £0.02 per scan
  • Total of the two calculations

The calculation updates as the figures are entered.

💷 Delay Repay

Use this page to help a customer access Delay Repay information.

The page contains a scannable QR code which can be shown to the customer for them to scan with their phone.

The page also explains that customers may be entitled to compensation where their CrossCountry train has been delayed by 30 minutes or more to the destination detailed on their ticket.

📋 TIR Builder

The TIR Builder helps capture information and prepare a structured TIR report.

Enter the relevant train information, irregularity, passenger details and answers to the questions. Additional operational notes can also be recorded.

The builder provides Copy, Email, Save TXT and Save PDF options.

The PDF is formatted in an XC style and includes the report timestamp and Page X of Y. The PDF filename uses:

NAME-TIR-DDMMYYYY.pdf

📄 PDF reports

Where a PDF option is provided, it exports the relevant completed report in an XC-themed format.

PDF reports include an XC footer with Page X of Y and the report generation time and date.

PDF generation is not provided on the Commission / Scan Calculator, Delay Repay or Help / Guide pages.

Remember

  • Check every entry before copying, emailing, texting or submitting a report.
  • Use the correct company mailbox, reporting system and operational procedure.
  • This app provides templates and prompts; it does not replace company procedures, formal reporting systems or emergency arrangements.
  • For emergencies or safety-critical matters, follow the appropriate operational process immediately.
